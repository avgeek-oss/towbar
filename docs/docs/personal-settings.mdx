Open **Settings → Account Settings**. The menu under your name also links to Profile, Preferences, Email & Password, and API Keys.

These settings affect your own account. Your team role determines which operational actions you can take, but every user can secure their account and review their sessions.

## Profile

Use **Profile → Display name** to change the name shown in the sidebar and team member list. Changing it does not change your sign-in email or permissions.

## Preferences

Open **Preferences → Date and time** to choose a date format, time format, and time zone. Search for a city such as `Kolkata` or choose `UTC`. The preview shows how your selection will appear. Select **Save** to apply it across Towbar.

Absolute dates and times in tables, charts, logs, and tooltips use these settings. Relative labels such as "5 minutes ago" are unchanged. The default is `16 Sept 2026`, `14:30`, and UTC. Your preferences follow your account, including across browsers and devices.

Custom monitoring ranges use your saved format and time zone. If a time occurs twice during a daylight-saving change, choose the first or second occurrence. Towbar rejects times skipped by a clock change.

Your personal API keys and MCP connections use your current preferences. Team API keys always use the default formats in UTC. Changing display preferences does not change scheduled operations or stored timestamps. See the [API localization contract](/api-reference/overview#date-and-time-localization).

### Change your email

1. Enter the new address under **Email address** and request a confirmation link.
2. Open the email sent to that address and follow the link within one hour.
3. Sign in again with the new address after confirmation.

Your current address stays active until you confirm. A newer request replaces the previous link. You can cancel a pending change from Email & Password. After confirmation, Towbar signs out your browser sessions and sends a notice to the old address.

The Towbar operator must configure [SMTP](/docs/integrations/notifications/email) in the control-plane runtime before Towbar can send the confirmation. Admins cannot replace another member's email from the Members page; the account holder must complete this flow.

### Change your password

Confirm with your passkey, then enter the new password twice. Accounts without a passkey also enter their current password. Use at least 15 characters. Password managers and paste are supported. Towbar checks new passwords against a list of known compromised passwords. If the check is unavailable, try again later.

After host-based password recovery, replace the temporary password during sign-in before opening the dashboard.

## Sessions

The Sessions table lists your active browser sessions, when each was last used, and when it expires. **This browser** identifies your current session. Revoke a session when you no longer use or recognize it.

Choose **Sign out** from the account menu to end the current browser session. Signing out does not revoke your personal API keys.

## Passkeys

Open **Account Settings → Passkeys** to add, rename, or remove a passkey. Use **Sign in with Passkey** to sign in without a password. Password sign-in also requires a passkey when one is registered.

Towbar requires device verification, such as a fingerprint, face scan, or PIN. Biometric data stays on the device. Sensitive actions use your passkey; accounts without a passkey confirm their password.

Adding your first passkey shows ten recovery codes. Copy or download them and store them securely. Each code works once, together with your password, if your passkeys are unavailable. **Replace recovery codes** in the page menu invalidates the previous set. Existing passkey users can generate codes there too.

Removing the last passkey returns the account to password sign-in and removes its recovery codes. Removing a passkey from Towbar does not delete its copy from your password manager.

Passkeys require HTTPS in production and belong to the site's domain. Changing that domain requires new passkeys; localhost is supported for development.

## Personal API keys

Open **API Keys** to create and revoke keys for REST API scripts. Choose the narrowest access and a suitable expiry. Copy the token when it is created; Towbar cannot show it again.

Your role limits what each key can do. Viewer keys are read-only. A role change can reduce an existing key's access. Read [API key permissions](/docs/api/authentication#keys-and-permissions) before choosing access for automation.

## MCP Guide

The **MCP Guide** page shows the endpoint and client configuration for this Towbar installation. Connect by signing in and approving access through OAuth. MCP does not accept API keys. Signed-in connections appear on the separate **MCP Connections** page below API Keys and expire after 30 days. This page shows client details and approved permissions, with a revoke action for each connection. See [Connect an MCP client](/docs/api/mcp).

## If you cannot sign in

Use **Forgot password** when SMTP is available. If you lost your passkeys, sign in with your password and an unused recovery code. An installation operator can follow [admin account recovery](/docs/self-hosting/upgrades#admin-account-recovery) when the usual recovery methods are unavailable.
