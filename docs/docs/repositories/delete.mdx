---
title: "Delete a repository"
description: "Disconnect syncing without losing workloads or history."
---

A repository with workloads or retained deployment history cannot be deleted. Disconnect its environments to stop syncing while preserving runtime, credentials and history.

To remove a workload, delete its manifest in Git and sync. Review it under **Retirements**, then explicitly confirm permanent removal if you no longer need its runtime or storage.

To move workloads to another repository, keep their IDs and environment names. Follow [conflicts and retirements](/docs/repositories/conflicts-and-retirements). Deleting a repository is not an ownership transfer.

An empty repository with no retained workloads or deployment history can be deleted after its active operations finish.
