`towbar_source_discover`

Read towbar.yml on a discovery branch to find declared environments before connecting. Does not create a source or deploy.

## Access and behavior

Choose **Administrator** when approving the [MCP connection](/docs/api/mcp). Your current team role and approved permissions constrain access.

Required permissions: `repository.connect`.
